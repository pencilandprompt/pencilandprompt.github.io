The verdict

HIT

VERDICT: HIT — with one catch (on W010's carry-forward: turn the flat face sculpted via peak highlights + core shadows). Sculpting hit. Peak highlights on forehead/nose/cheekbone finally land and real core shadows give form — the face reads lit and three-dimensional, the best form-modeling in the series, and the shadows stayed WARM this time (the W009 grey lesson held). The catch — overcorrected again (the standing habit). The brief called for soft frontal light with modest shadows, but the orbital/eye-socket darks went heavy and harsh, reading hollow/raccoon — the "shadows too heavy" thread (W005/W008), and the same overshoot-past-the-middle as the W009 redraw. Forehead highlights read scratchy/streaky (scar-like) rather than smooth lit passes. Strong: warm unified skin on the measured L*≈53 tone; hair, likeness and toned ground all strong.

What the next week targets

Calibration/restraint is the thread now: you hit the sculpting, so pull the orbital + jaw shadows back to MID-depth (lighter than instinct) and match the reference's softness instead of overshooting each note. Also smooth the forehead highlight passes so they read as light, not scratches.
