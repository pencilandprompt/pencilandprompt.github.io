The verdict

LOGGED

Skin reads flat, not lit. The guide's whole thesis is a luminous layered build (warm-grey base → siennas → peach/cream highlights), but the lit planes — forehead apex, cheekbone peaks, nose bridge — never reach a true highlight, so the face looks evenly filled rather than modeled by a light source. Darks went grey (ties to W005). Last loop the darks were too raw and hot; this time they swung the other way — under-eye hollows, nose-side and beard shadow read neutral/flat, the exact "dead grey" the brief warns against. The dial overshot the warm-luminous middle. Beard is a flat mass (recurring vs W005 stubble). Even darkness with no dense-at-jaw → sparse-at-cheek-edge gradient, so it sits on the face instead of growing from skin — second loop running. Strong: likeness, symmetry, eye catch-lights solid; skin is more unified than W005 (real progress); braids well-structured but one flat valu

What the next week targets

Carry-forward thread is darks calibration: W005 too hot → W006 too grey, so next loop aim deliberately for the warm-but-controlled middle and push the light-zone to an actual highlight so the form reads as lit. One isolated drill: a beard-and-jaw study working only density gradient + warm (not grey) shadow — kill the recurring beard-mass flaw before the next full face.
